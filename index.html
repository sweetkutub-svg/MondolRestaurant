Our Heritage

Two decades of
flavour & soul

Mondol Restaurant was born from a simple conviction — that every meal should be a memory. Founded in 2005 by Chef Arjun Mondol, we've spent two decades perfecting the marriage of Bengal's rich culinary heritage with contemporary technique.

Our kitchen is guided by the seasons, sourced from trusted farmers, and executed with reverence. The result is food that nourishes not just the body, but the spirit.

Meet the Chef
